# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 3, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how DrillFit ("we," "us," or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the DrillFit iOS application. By using DrillFit, you agree to the practices described below.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve the app's features:

- **Account information** — your email address and name, provided when you create an account.
- **Workout data** — exercises, sets, reps, weights, workout history, and personal records you log.
- **Nutrition logs** — meals, foods, calories, macronutrients, micronutrients, water intake, and supplements you record.
- **Body measurements** — weight, height, body-fat percentage, and related metrics you enter.
- **Apple Health data** — health and fitness metrics (such as steps, active energy, exercise minutes, heart rate, sleep, weight, and workouts) that you explicitly grant us permission to read.
- **Community content** — posts, comments, reactions, and profile details you choose to share in community features.

We do **not** collect this data unless you actively provide it or grant the relevant permission.

## 2. How Your Data Is Stored

- **Cloud storage (Firebase):** Your account, profile, community content, and synced fitness data are stored using Google Firebase services — **Firebase Authentication** (login/identity), **Cloud Firestore** (data), and **Firebase Storage** (images such as profile pictures).
- **On your device (Isar):** Much of your day-to-day data — workouts, nutrition logs, and measurements — is stored locally on your device in an on-device Isar database. Local data remains on your device and is removed when you delete the app or reset your data within the app.

## 3. Third-Party Services

DrillFit relies on the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

- **Firebase (Google LLC)** — authentication, database, and file storage. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Spoonacular API** — used to look up nutrition and restaurant food data. Food search queries may be sent to Spoonacular to return results. See: https://spoonacular.com/food-api/terms
- **Apple HealthKit** — used to read health and fitness data on your device with your permission (see Section 4). See Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

- DrillFit reads Apple Health data **only after you grant explicit permission** through the iOS Health permission prompt. You can review or revoke this access at any time in the iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → DrillFit**.
- Apple Health data is used solely to display your activity and progress within the app.
- Apple Health data is **never** sold, shared with, or transmitted to any third party, and it is **never** used for advertising.

## 5. Community Features

If you choose to use community features, your **posts, comments, reactions, username, and profile information are visible to other DrillFit users**. Do not share information in the community that you wish to keep private. You are responsible for the content you post.

## 6. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

## 7. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at **support@drillfit.com**. Upon a verified request, we will delete your account, cloud-stored data, and uploaded files. You can also clear locally stored data at any time from within the app (Settings → Reset All Data) or by deleting the app from your device.

## 8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including the security controls provided by Firebase, to protect your information in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children's Privacy

DrillFit is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may be communicated within the app. Your continued use of DrillFit after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
